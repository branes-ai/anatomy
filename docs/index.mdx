## What Anatomy is

**Anatomy** is the mission-framework tier of the Branes.AI platform. The
platform repos are the nervous system: [Cortex](https://branes-ai.github.io/cortex/)
perceives and plans, [Reflex](https://branes-ai.github.io/reflex/) controls and
keeps the vehicle safe, and [Synapse](https://github.com/branes-ai/synapse) runs
learned models. Anatomy is the body plan they are assembled into. Every mission
repo, such as [Racing Drone](https://branes-ai.github.io/racing-drone/), builds
its vehicle on it.

Anatomy holds no algorithms and no mission. It holds the mechanism every
mission needs: what a component is, how components are composed, how a run is
logged and replayed, and how it is simulated and scored. A mission supplies its
task: its world, the components that see it, and how its runs are scored.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Slots and composition" icon="puzzle">
		Components fill fixed slots, defined as C++20 concepts. A baseline names
		one implementation per slot, and the generator composes them at compile
		time.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Deterministic replay" icon="approve-check">
		Integer-nanosecond time and a log of every input and output, so any run
		replays record for record.
	</Card>
	<Card title="A closed-loop simulator" icon="rocket">
		SITL on the reflex dynamics, with a simulated flight controller, IMU,
		faults and camera timing. The mission adds its oracle sensors and its
		scorer.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Any scalar type" icon="setting">
		Every contract and slot is templated on its scalar type, so a stack runs
		in `float`, posits or bfloat16 for mixed-precision study.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## The tiers at a glance

| Tier | Repos | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Mission | `racing-drone`, `scout-drone`, `nano-drone`, `isr-drone`, `interceptor-drone`, `counter-uas` | The vehicle and its task: world, task slots, stack, scorer, data |
| **Mission framework** | **`anatomy`** | **Contracts, slots and composition, configs, the log, the harness, the simulator** |
| Platform | `cortex`, `synapse`, `reflex` | The algorithms: perception and planning, learned models, control and safety |
| Math | MTL5, Universal | Linear algebra and computer arithmetic |
